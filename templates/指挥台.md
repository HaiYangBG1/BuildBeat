# Daily work

- Prepare the goal, scope, acceptance and plan in work.md, and commit it.
- Accept once the owner has decided.
- Write the change and commit it; send the commit with review; inspect with status.
- Fix what the review found in a new commit and review again.
- The owner decides on the reviewed commit: approve it by its full commit id, or reject it with a reason.
- Diagnose the run config and a work's readiness with check.
- After the merge, write result.md and close the work.
