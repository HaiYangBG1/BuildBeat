# Product simplification

The owner accepted the feature-subtraction proposal and instructed implementation.
BuildBeat will focus on portable work context, a recoverable delivery loop, and
evidence-backed human decisions. The approved scope includes all three stages:
retire operations-specific features, consolidate the user surface, and replace
general workflow/policy authoring with a fixed software-delivery flow.

Preserve candidate/evidence/approval binding, read-only review, scope checks,
recovery, cache, parallel isolation, notification, and regression coverage.
Do not modify target projects, discard historical records, merge, push, publish,
or change installed runtimes as part of this work.

Acceptance: packaged first run, core regressions, migration rejection and legacy
readback, bilingual current documentation, and independent review must pass.
Review budget: six rounds for this Work; no automatic acceptance of a candidate.
