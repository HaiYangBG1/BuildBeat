# Code of conduct

Solobaton contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project interactions respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is specific, evidence-based, and directed at the work rather than the person.

The maintainer may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary. Use GitHub's content-reporting controls for platform abuse. Do not publish private information while reporting conduct concerns.
